## about
learn japanese while you pretend to do your job with **[kana.build](https://www.kana.build/)** - a flashcard style learning tool designed to help you master hiragana and katakana. only words that contain the kana characters you select will be displayed. choose a wordlist that fits your level, ranging from  beginner lists like `animals` to all levels of the JLPT and even the dictionary. **がんばって**!
